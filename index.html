OrchString.
One platform for end-to-end business automation.

OrchString is an AI-powered, enterprise-oriented execution platform designed to transform business objectives into coordinated, controlled, and verifiable workflows.

orchstring is being developed as a complete enterprise software platform for end-to-end business automation. It connects people, applications, data and AI across planning, approvals, execution, verification and recovery.

Business use cases under development include AI workflow automation, multi-agent orchestration, accounts payable and invoice processing, customer onboarding, service requests, procurement coordination, recurring reporting and compliance reviews. Each pilot is scoped to available integrations, organizational policies and human approval requirements.

ONE ILLUSTRATIVE BUSINESS CASE

Potential capacity gains in supplier invoice processing

Illustrative scenario · Not a claim of measured OrchString performance

What if your finance team gained back 750 hours each month?

Consider 10,000 invoices per month. If a configured solution handles 75% of those invoices and avoids 6 minutes of manual work per invoice, the estimated capacity freed is 750 staff-hours monthly.

At an assumed loaded labor cost of ₹300 per hour, that represents ₹2.25 lakh in potential gross monthly labor value (₹27 lakh annually), before AI, infrastructure, integration, support and review costs.

06 / CORE CAPABILITIES

Built for real work.

Goal-driven agents

Agents operate around explicit objectives, lifecycle states, cancellation, retry, replanning and verification rather than an unbounded chat loop.

Multi-task orchestration

Jobs can contain independent and dependent work, enabling deterministic task graphs, parallel execution and partial recovery.

Model-independent runtime

Models are replaceable execution resources. Routing can account for capability, hardware, latency, privacy, cost and policy.

Capability-first tools

Intent maps to required capabilities, then to permitted implementations. orchstring is not hard-coded to one application.

Evidence & verification

Observation, criticism, reasoning and verification create an evidence trail instead of trusting model output blindly.

Recovery & fencing

Execution attempts, leases and fencing protect durable state when workers fail, restart or are replaced.

Security by boundary

Discovery never grants permission. Consequential actions flow through authorization, policy, risk and audit controls.

OS agnostic

orchstring can run on ordinary Linux and other supported systems while leaving room for deep native underlying platform integration.

Developer intelligence

Software engineering is a first-class workload: inspect, reason, modify, build, test, debug, benchmark and verify.

Business work keeps moving.

What if something interrupts the workflow?

Rather than lose track of the entire process, a well-configured workflow can pause affected steps, preserve progress, request human help, retry safely and continue once the required conditions are met.

Less operational disruption. Clearer accountability. More predictable handoffs.
