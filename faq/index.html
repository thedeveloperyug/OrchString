OrchString FAQ

Find direct answers about the platform design, business workflow examples, integration scope and how to discuss an orchstring pilot.

By the orchstring team · Updated 26 October 2026

What is OrchString?

orchstring is an AI-powered, enterprise-oriented execution platform being developed as complete enterprise software for end-to-end business automation. It brings people, applications, data and AI together across planning, approvals, execution, verification and recovery.

What business workflows is it designed to support?

orchstring is designed around reusable orchestration patterns for invoice processing, customer onboarding, service requests, purchasing, recurring reporting and evidence reviews. Actual workflow support depends on the integrations, capabilities and policies configured for a deployment.

How is it different from a chatbot?

A chatbot primarily provides a conversational response. orchstring is designed to coordinate the steps around a business objective: planning tasks, discovering capabilities, authorizing actions, observing execution and verifying results. A model response can be one step inside that workflow.

Can it connect Gmail, Outlook, Teams and Google Drive?

These services, along with Google and Microsoft accounts, are examples of integration targets shown on the website. Connector availability, account permissions and setup depend on the deployment. Confirm the required integrations with the team before planning a production workflow.

Does it work with different AI models?

The platform design treats models as replaceable execution resources. It aims to coordinate local or cloud models according to capabilities, privacy needs, hardware, latency and cost constraints. Specific providers and model support must be confirmed for the selected deployment.

Can it process invoices or make payments automatically?

The invoice example covers extraction, purchase order matching, duplicate checks, exception handling and approval workflows. AI does not independently release payments. Financial actions must respect customer permissions, policies and human approval requirements.

How are approvals and results verified?

The design places authorization before consequential execution and uses observations, evidence and checks to assess the result. A reviewer can approve an action when required, while subsequent verification checks the outcome. The exact controls depend on the workflow and deployment.

Is OrchString available, and where can I find pricing?

orchstring is in active development. The website does not publish standard pricing, a service-level agreement or a guarantee that every illustrated feature is available in every deployment. Contact the sales team to confirm current release support, pilot scope, integrations and commercial terms.

Who is building OrchString?

Yogesh Pandey is the founder and creator. Rishabh Saluja is the co-founder focused on business and growth. The team information and professional profile links are published in the People section of the official website.

How do I explore the console or discuss a pilot?

Use the Explore OrchString Console link on the homepage to visit the current console destination. For a pilot, describe the business process, input sources, systems involved, approval rules and desired outcome using the sales contact on the official website.

Development status

orchstring is in active development. Workflow examples describe the platform direction; available connectors, clients and controls depend on release and deployment support. Confirm your requirements with the team.
